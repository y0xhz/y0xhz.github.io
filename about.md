  <div class="prose">
    <p class="lede">I’m y0xhz. I work in offensive security, and I’m based in Indonesia.</p>
    <p>My work spans enterprise networks, cloud infrastructure, and mobile applications. I test how systems can be compromised, document what I find, and help teams understand where to make changes.</p>
    <p>I’m also a guest lecturer and cybersecurity consultant at <a href="https://hacktrace.id">HackTrace</a>, teaching penetration testing and red team operations.</p>
    <p>Outside that work, I build tools in Rust and design CTF challenges. This site is a place to keep the useful parts: working notes, small projects, and lessons from the lab.</p>
    <h2>What I work on</h2>
    <ul class="discipline-list">
      <li><strong>Networks & Active Directory</strong><span>Internal penetration testing, attack paths, and enterprise environments.</span></li>
      <li><strong>Malware research & reverse engineering</strong><span>Windows internals, PE packers, cryptography, and detection research.</span></li>
      <li><strong>Mobile & cloud security</strong><span>Android and iOS applications, AWS, and GCP assessments.</span></li>
      <li><strong>OSINT & bug bounty</strong><span>Reconnaissance, investigation, and application security research.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <h2>Learning in public</h2>
    <p>I play CTFs for fun and learning. I’m interested in the steps that make a solution click, and in turning those steps into notes someone else can use.</p>
    <p>Some of the work here is unfinished. I keep that status visible rather than presenting a draft as a finished tool or article.</p>
